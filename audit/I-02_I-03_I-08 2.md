# I-02, I-03, I-08: America First MOUs. Fixed

**What changed.**
- America First MOUs now follows each country's full MOU schedule, year by year, exactly as written.
- It applies only to the 16 countries that have both a signed MOU and schedule data.
- MOU status now comes from KFF's tracker, not from whether the team sheet has a schedule.

**Step 0 (checked first).** The year-varying cut logic (`cut_path` per year) and cohort tracking of people off ART
(`art_cohorts`) are on this branch.

## Behaviour

**Donor Scenario dropdown**, on the Dashboard and Validation pages (shared helper `donor_scenario_select`):

| Group | What the dropdown does |
|---|---|
| A | America First MOUs is available |
| B | Listed as "America First MOUs (MOU signed; no funding data available.)". Choosing it keeps the previous scenario and shows "America First MOUs: MOU signed; no funding data available." No model run, no averages, no other country's values |
| C | Not listed |

- **Switching country:** moving to a B or C country while the preset is selected falls back to Full US Exit with
  "{Country}: no MOU funding data; showing Full US Exit."
- **The model enforces it too:** `run_country` raises an error for a non-A country under this preset
  (`Scenario.mou_only`), and `run_all` includes only group A.

**The schedule (group A):**
- cut(year) = 1 - mou_us_{year} / mou_us_ref, applied to US bilateral (direct-channel) aid each year through
  `Scenario.cuts_by_year`, which `run_country` now uses for the money stage. Levels are used directly, never rescaled.
- A funding increase counts as a 0% cut (Kenya 2026).
- Schedules that ease later (Ethiopia 2027, Rwanda 2027, Botswana 2027) are followed as written. People return to care
  through the cohort logic: Ethiopia has fewer people off ART in 2027 than in 2026 (tested).
- Botswana and South Sudan's schedules end in 2028; the 2028 cut is held through 2030. Both the caption and the chart
  say so.
- The US multilateral exits are unchanged (Global Fund 23%, WHO, Gavi and UNFPA 100%) and constant over the years.

**Removed:**
- `DEFAULT_MOU_YEAR`, the MOU Year control, the non-MOU average cut (`non_mou_cut`, `mou_average_cut_pct`), `mou_cuts`,
  `mou_cut_path`, and the "MOU schedule" option in How Funding Changes.
- A grep of all app and model code finds none of them. Only `audit/analysis.py`, which records the earlier audit,
  refers to them; it now refuses to run on this commit.

**On screen:**
- **Scenario box:**
  - the scenario description, e.g. "US bilateral aid cut: 0% (2026), 4%, 16%, 20%, 60% (2030), per Kenya's MOU";
  - a small red line chart of the yearly cuts;

- **Kicker:** "AMERICA FIRST MOUS · FOLLOWS MOU SCHEDULE · {GOVERNMENT RESPONSE}".
- **Gradual budget increase:** the year panel shows the schedule as written, with no cut sliders.
- **Comparison:** Scenario B never applies Scenario A's funding path to an MOU schedule.
- **How the Damage Scales:** the dashed "this scenario, scaled" path is not drawn for this preset, because it would
  rescale the schedule.
- **All Countries:** only group A is included, under the heading and pill "MOU Countries With Funding Data (16)". On the
  map, group B is light grey with hover "MOU signed; no funding data", and group C is lighter grey with hover "No MOU".
- **Validation:** the preset's row says "(16 MOU Countries With Funding Data)".
- **Methods:** cites KFF (as of 2026-10-08) as the source of MOU status, and describes the new rules.

## Tests

`tests/test_mou.py` (new, 55 tests):
- the KFF list has 36 countries, and groups A/B/C are as above;
- header status comes from the KFF list only;
- for every group-A country, every US bilateral cell's applied cut in every year equals the schedule exactly. The US
  multilateral exits are unchanged, and `run_country` reports the schedule and the matching yearly aid lost;
- the rules hold: Kenya 2026 is 0%; Ethiopia's easing is followed; Botswana and South Sudan hold 2028; Ethiopia's people
  off ART fall in 2027;
- groups B and C raise an error, and the all-country run (and precomputed file) contains only group A;
- nothing rescales a schedule: the old helpers and the MOU path option are gone, and every schedule equals the sheet's
  own levels.

**Test baseline.**
- `tests/baseline_sudden.json` locks in each preset's sudden-cut results, made by the model before the later
  mechanisms.
- America First MOUs was redefined, so the old model can't produce it. HND and IRQ (groups B and C) can no longer run
  it at all.
- Its entries were therefore regenerated with `python tests/make_baseline.py --mou-only`: the current model, the later
  mechanisms switched off (as test (a) runs it), for KEN, NGA and MOZ.
- The other 90 entries are unchanged. The MOU entries now guard the schedule-driven results against unintended change;
  `test_mou.py` checks the schedule itself.
- Result: 215 of 215 tests pass.

**Automated checks** (`audit/checks.py`, now run for group A only under this preset):
- 1,236 America First MOUs rows. The only failure is a sub-one-person rounding difference between the hero and the
  table (Eswatini, known under I-15).
