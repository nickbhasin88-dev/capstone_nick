## W-9 Thresholds: the cut at which coverage crosses a target

A constant cut (the same fraction in every year 2026-2030) to US direct-channel aid only (no multilateral exits), scanned from 0% to 100% in 1% steps. Coverage after = baseline coverage - people losing the service / people in need (the model's definition; ART among all people living with HIV, TB treatment = WHO case detection, DTP3 among births). Thresholds: ART 95% and 90%, TB treatment 90% (End TB), DTP3 90% (IA2030). 'Floor' is coverage at a 100% cut: it is bounded by the donor-funded pool (external share of spending x people covered) and the 15% continuity assumption, so a country can be unable to reach a threshold at any cut.
