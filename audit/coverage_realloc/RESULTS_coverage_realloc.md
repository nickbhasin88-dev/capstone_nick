Generated by `audit/coverage_realloc/run_coverage_realloc.py`. Engine and conventions as `audit/reallocation/run_reallocation_2027.py`: America First MOUs preset, **US bilateral MOU schedule only** (the preset's US exits from WHO, Gavi, UNFPA and 23% of the Global Fund switched off in memory), dashboard defaults (no government response, sudden path, death-rate trends on), 400 draws, seed 7, central run plus paired 2.5-97.5 ranges. Only the 2027 US MOU amount changes, in memory. 'People on service' are the model's full-cost equivalents in 2027 after the 15% continuity assumption: ART patient-years, TB courses, bednets, people sprayed, malaria cases treated, children vaccinated. 'ART coverage pp' = ART patient-years lost / people living with HIV x 100. Deaths 2026-2030 are shown as a secondary column.

With constant marginals the optimum is bang-bang: the best countries are filled to their reference and the worst cut to the floor.
