# I-07. Fixed: user-set Gavi and Global Fund cuts

## 1. How the old values were derived

The preset applied fixed channel cuts to every donor's money through each fund (`scenarios.py`,
`sc.chan = {"Global Fund": 0.28, "Gavi": 0.24}`):

| Fund | Old cut | Derivation | What it actually compares |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global Fund | 28% | 1 - US$11.34B / US$15.7B = 27.8% | Eighth Replenishment total **at the pledging summit** (Nov 2025) vs the Seventh Replenishment (US$15.7B). The final total is US$12.64B (Global Fund Board, Feb 2026), so the same comparison now gives **19.5%**; against the US$18B target it gives **29.8%** |
| Gavi | 24% | 1 - about US$9B / US$11.9B = 24.4% | Money raised for Gavi 6.0 (2026-2030) vs **the target**, not vs the previous cycle. Gavi 5.0 (2021-2025) raised US$8.8B, so pledges are about 2% **higher** than the previous cycle. The 24% is a shortfall against plans, not a fall in spending |

Both cuts were applied to each fund's 2021-23 disbursements (the model baseline), so the Gavi figure in particular did
not measure what it was applied to.

## 3. Change

- **Preset.** "Global Fund & Gavi Shortfalls" now cuts every source donor's money through Gavi and through the Global
  Fund by values the user sets, against each fund's 2021-23 disbursements (the model baseline).
  - Defaults: **Gavi 15%, Global Fund 20%** (`scenarios.GAVI_CUT_DEFAULT`, `GF_CUT_DEFAULT`; `shortfall_cuts`,
    `shortfall_label`, `preset_description`).
  - This preset has no US multilateral exit, so no money is cut twice. The US share of each fund is cut once, at the
    channel rate (tested). Other presets are unchanged.
- **Section 4 control box.** When the preset is selected, it shows two sliders: "Gavi Funding Cut (%)" and "Global Fund
  Funding Cut (%)", each 0-50% in 1% steps.
  - Each slider has small reference marks underneath:
    - Gavi: "~15%: fall vs 2021-23 spending (our estimate)"; "24%: gap vs Gavi's US$11.9B target".
    - Global Fund: "15%: allocation cut in MOU countries (KFF, Jun 2026)"; "~20%: pledges vs previous replenishment";
      "~24%: median cut in allocation letters (Aidspan)"; "30%: gap vs US$18B target".
  - Each has a tooltip with the derivation and sources.
  - The Scenario B options in the comparison get their own pair of sliders.
- **Text that follows the chosen values.**
  - Kicker: "Funding-Cut Scenario · Global Fund & Gavi Shortfalls · Gavi −15% · Global Fund −20% · …".
  - Headline: "If Gavi funding falls 15% and Global Fund funding 20%, {country} could see …". With one cut at 0 it names
    only the other fund.
  - Also updated: the Section 4 caption, the Scenario A/B labels, the Validation and Methods captions, and the preset
    description (tooltip and Validation), which adds "Selected: Gavi −x% · Global Fund −y%."
- **All Countries** always uses the defaults (precomputed file, rebuilt at 15% / 20%). If the sliders have been moved,
  one line says: "This page uses the default cuts (Gavi −15% · Global Fund −20%). Your setting (…) applies to the
  country Dashboard only."
- **Methods** preset table row rewritten with the derivations and sources. The missing full stop in the IHME 2025 row
  (from the I-15 edit) is fixed too.
