# I-09, I-27 (PMTCT part) and I-04. Fixed

## I-09: HIV testing and care money counted as patients on ART

**Change.**
- In `prepare_model.py`, `hiv_art` now uses only IHME "HIV - Treatment" (`hiv_treat`).
- A new line, `hiv_care_ct` ("HIV Testing & Care"), takes "Care and Support" (`hiv_care`) and "Counseling and
  Testing" (`hiv_ct`).
- The new line has no units of its own. It is treated exactly like health-systems money (`health_model.LINKED`):
  - its pool is its own money plus its share of unspecified and systems money (by the country's mix);
  - kappa x the lost pool is added to ART's money lost, so it adds people losing ART;
  - the rest is non-service money.
- Wording used throughout: "50% of lost testing and care money is counted as lost ART care (assumption; range
  25%-90%)". The values are read from `hss_kappa` in `model_params.csv` (`model_section.care_ct_note`).
- Whether ART donor money buys more than everyone covered is judged on ART's own (treatment) money only (`cap_base` in
  `_units_lost`).
- **Service table:** a "HIV Testing & Care" row under HIV shows its money. Cost, people losing the service and
  coverage show "–", and deaths show "In HIV Treatment", each with a tooltip explaining that it works through ART.
- **Methods:** program-area table, the testing-and-care paragraph and the HIV text are updated.

**Money check.** New `hiv_art` + `hiv_care_ct` equals the old `hiv_art` in every country x donor x channel cell
(3,299 cells, 70 countries). The largest difference is US$0.00000006 (float). Every other line is unchanged.

**Share of the former ART-line money moved to Testing & Care:**
- median 40.6%, from 13.2% (South Africa) to 59.8% (Guyana);
- US$760M of US$2,354M a year in total;
- Kenya 40.9%, Nigeria 38.2%, Mozambique 32.3%, Uganda 31.1%, Zambia 31.8%, Malawi 18.3%, Honduras 37.0%.

**Donor-funded people on ART vs PEPFAR (new on the Validation page).**
- Model figure: the smaller of the people on ART (outside PMTCT) and ART money / cost, summed. It falls from
  **11.21M to 7.78M**.
- PEPFAR reports supporting about **20.6M**.
- PEPFAR counts everyone at sites it supports, including people whose drugs the government or the Global Fund buys.
  The model counts full-cost equivalents paid for by donors, so the PEPFAR figure is larger by design.

## I-27 (PMTCT part): PMTCT cost

**Cost.** `uc_pmtct` = US$250 (150-450) per HIV-positive pregnant woman per year under Option B+, including her ART,
testing, infant prophylaxis and infant diagnosis. Status "sourced": Health Policy and Planning 2016;31(8):1133
review. The unit reads "per woman per year, including her ART" in the service table, Methods and the cost table.

**Double counting: how it was checked.**
1. **Dollars.**
   - In the raw IHME file (`elim_ch == 0`, 2021-23, 147,843 rows), the 11 HIV program-area columns add up to
     `hiv_dah_23` in every row, within IHME's US$1K-per-cell rounding (largest difference US$4K; totals US$24.31B vs
     US$24.29B).
   - `prepare_model.LINES` maps each HIV program area to exactly one line (tested).
   - So no dollar is in both PMTCT (`hiv_pmtct`) and ART (`hiv_treat`).
2. **Deaths.** The PMTCT line counts only infant deaths (transmission averted x infant mortality). Mothers' deaths are
   not added (conservative), so no death is counted in both lines.
3. **Women: this was double counted, and is now fixed.**
   - People on ART (ART coverage x PLHIV) includes pregnant women on Option B+, whose ART is now in the PMTCT cost and
     paid from PMTCT money.
   - The ART line's money could therefore "lose" women whose ART PMTCT money pays for.
   - Fix: the ART line's people covered (for its cap and its donor-money test) = people on ART minus women covered by
     PMTCT (`pmtct_covered`).
   - Effect: under Full US Exit, people losing ART change in 13 countries, where ART was capped. The largest are South
     Sudan (41,593 → 39,480; deaths 7,341 → 6,966) and Haiti (85,308 → 83,244).

**PMTCT lines where donor money > cost x women covered: 50 → 45** (of 69 with PMTCT aid). ART lines: 28 → 14,
because testing and care money no longer counts toward ART's own-money test.

## I-04: per-US$1M cards

- **Method.** Each card runs the full model for a US$1M-a-year loss to one disease:
  - every row of that disease is cut by min(1, US$1M / its aid), so the loss is spread over all its lines in
    proportion to current aid, including HIV testing & care, unspecified and systems money;
  - no government replacement, sudden path;
  - every mechanism is included: caps, TB spread, malaria rebound and HIV infection deaths.
  - Code: `health_model.per_million_card`, cached in `model_section._card_runs`.
- **What each card shows.** People and coverage drop are for the main service (ART, TB treatment, bednets, routine
  vaccines); deaths are for the whole disease, 2026-2030. Where a disease's aid is under US$1M, all of it is cut and
  the results are scaled to US$1M (said in the footer).
- **Labels.**
  - Title: "What Every US$1 Million Cut Does, by Disease"; card titles HIV / TB / Malaria / Immunization.
  - Footer: "Spread across all {disease} services in proportion to current aid", plus the I-24 wording "About $X of
    aid lost per life lost (based on US$1M a year for 5 years)".
  - No aid: "No {disease} aid in {country}" (tested).
- **Check.** New check in `audit/checks.py`, `card_deaths_equal_full_model_1M_uniform_cut`, for every country x
  disease: **280 / 280 pass**.
