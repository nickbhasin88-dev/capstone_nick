# 2027 MOU reallocation, dollar for dollar, HIV money only: 15 countries (no model changes)

Generated by `audit/reallocation/run_dollar_hiv_2027.py` from `allocations_2027_v2.csv`. Every dollar moved between countries is treated as HIV money: US$1 of MOU = US$1 of HIV aid, spent the way the country's US HIV aid is spent (ART, PMTCT, HIV prevention, testing and care, OVC) at the model's unit costs. No cut fraction, no `mou_us_ref` leverage, no 0% floor: money above the Original MOU amount restores what the Original MOU cut and then raises coverage above the 2021-23 baseline, capped at 100% of need. The 2027 total is the same in every ceiling (US$2,469,904,824; the ceilings differ from it by at most US$0.01). Conversions come from the model's marginal run (`marginal_2027_v2.csv`) divided by the HIV share of each country's US direct aid; HIV deaths and infections are a linear approximation at central parameters.
