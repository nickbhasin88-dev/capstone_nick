# Best reallocation of the 2027 US MOU money for deaths, 15 countries (no model changes)

Generated by `audit/reallocation/run_optimal_realloc_2027.py`. Dollar for dollar: US$1 of MOU moved = US$1 of US direct aid in the country, spent the way that country's US direct aid is spent, run through the model itself (America First MOUs, US bilateral schedule only, dashboard defaults; deaths ranges from 400 paired draws).

**Bounds.** Total unchanged; a receiving country can at most get back its 2021-23 US direct aid (0% cut in 2027: the model has no coverage above baseline, so it is not extrapolated); a giving country can at most give the US aid it still has; each country within +/-10%, 20% or 30% of its signed 2027 amount; one version also limits the total moved to the team's Higher Ceiling (US$133.6M).

**Method.** Each country's deaths 2026-2030 were computed with the model on a US$1M grid of aid changes. Starting from the Original MOU, US$1M was moved at a time between whichever two countries lowered total deaths the most, in either direction, until no move helped. The model's curves are not straight lines (restoring ART money brings back people cut in 2026; deeper cuts cost fewer deaths per dollar), so this is the best allocation found, not a proven global optimum. Amounts are in whole US$M.
