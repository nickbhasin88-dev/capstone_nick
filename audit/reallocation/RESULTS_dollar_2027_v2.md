# 2027 MOU reallocation, dollar for dollar: coverage, 15 countries (no model changes)

Generated by `audit/reallocation/run_dollar_realloc_2027.py` from `allocations_2027_v2.csv`. Each country's change in its 2027 MOU amount is taken at face value as a change in US direct aid (US$1 of MOU = US$1 of aid): no cut fraction, no `mou_us_ref` leverage, no 0% floor. Money above the Original MOU amount first restores the services the Original MOU cut and then raises coverage above the 2021-23 baseline. Dollars become people on each service through the model's own conversion for each country (people kept and deaths averted per US$1M of aid, from the marginal run in `marginal_2027_v2.csv`), extrapolated at the same rate beyond the Original MOU's level and capped at 100% of need. Same model run as the main report: America First MOUs, US bilateral schedule only, dashboard defaults, central parameters.
