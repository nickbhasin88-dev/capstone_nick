**Why the two statistics differ, and in both directions.**
- The uncertain parameters are drawn from asymmetric triangular distributions. For example, HIV transmission off ART is
  0.04 with a range of 0.02-0.08, so it is skewed upward.
- The median of the draws therefore drifts away from the run with every parameter at its central value.
- The direction depends on which skewed parameters drive a country's infections: transmission and the ART hazard pull
  the median up; unit-cost multipliers pull it either way.
- Deaths (all scenarios) are unchanged: they were already the central run.
