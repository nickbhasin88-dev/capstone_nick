# W-6. The government response needed to hold ART, TB treatment and vaccination flat (no model changes)

Generated by `audit/gov_response/run_gov_response.py`. America First MOUs preset, US bilateral MOU schedule only (US multilateral exits off; the peak-year loss with them on is shown for reference), dashboard defaults otherwise, 400 draws for the death ranges. For each country the smallest replacement share theta (the dashboard's 'Replace a set share', `health_model.Fiscal` mode custom) that leaves nobody off ART, TB treatment (DS and DR) or routine vaccination in any year 2026-2030, with the 'lives first' allocation (services refilled in the model's deaths-per-dollar order, so these three come first) and no fiscal-space cap; found by bisection (22 steps). Under the dashboard's default pro-rata allocation the whole loss has to be replaced (theta = 1) to hold any one service flat, because replacement is spread over every line in proportion to its loss. The cost is theta x the peak year's aid lost (usually 2030, when the MOU cuts are deepest).

## Step 3. Reading

- theta needed ranges from 0.30 (LBR) to 0.68 (NGA): the share of each country's lost US money that sits on treatment and vaccine lines (plus the lines the model ranks above them). The rest of the loss is prevention, testing, OVC, programme management and systems money, which this exercise lets go.
- As a share of GHES the peak-year cost runs from 0.6% (BWA) to 38.1% (MWI); countries above 10% would need more than a decade of their usual real GHES growth to absorb it, and more than the model's fiscal-space capacity in 10 of 15 countries.
- Caveats: GHES 2023 and revenue are IHME / World Bank levels in current US$; revenue is missing for Liberia, Nigeria and Sierra Leone; fiscal-space capacity is the model's labelled assumption (a strong year's extra GHES growth x a debt-stress factor); the 'lives first' allocation is a best case the dashboard labels as hypothetical; the MOU co-financing commitments are not modelled; costs are in the model's 2023 US$ while MOU amounts are nominal.
