# I-10. Fixed: Section 3 by level of government (display only)

## Change

`budget_section.py` builds each side's boxes; `views/1_Dashboard.py` draws them, and `audit/checks.py` checks them.

- **General-government breakdown** (IMF revenue data / COFOG coverage "General government"): used as is and scaled to
  the IMF WEO general-government total. They are the same level of government, so this only reconciles the two IMF
  sources.
- **Central-government breakdown:** shown at its own size and **never rescaled**, labelled "Central Government", plus
  one grey box for the rest of the WEO total, "Other Levels of Government (no breakdown)". The grey box is left out
  when it is under 0.005% of GDP (rounding).
- **Central total above the WEO total:** the central breakdown is shown alone, with the note "Central-government
  figures exceed the IMF WEO general-government total this year, so the central breakdown is shown alone." Example:
  Iraq revenue 2024, central 38.8% vs WEO 38.3% of GDP.
- **Labels and captions.**
  - Each side's label states its level, e.g. "Money In: Revenue (Central Government) + Borrowing" and "Money Out:
    Spending (General Government)".
  - If the sides differ, one caption reads: "Revenue and spending come from different levels of government in this
    year's data."
  - Another caption explains the grey box when there is one.
- **Pills:** still IMF WEO general-government revenue, spending and borrowing (unchanged).
- **Health:** the box is the COFOG health items at the side's size. The note when COFOG health is less than half of
  IHME government health spending is kept.
- **Methods:** Section 3 "How the treemap is built" is rewritten.
