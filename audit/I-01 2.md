# I-01: model aid baseline larger than IHME's totals. Fixed

**Cause:** IHME's `elim_ch` double-counting flag was not applied.

**Evidence.**
- The DAH codebook (`datasets/IHME_DAH_DATABASE_1990_2026_CB_Y2026M09D18.csv`) defines `elim_ch` as
  "1 = Drop to avoid double counting transfers between channels".
- The user guide (`..._USER_GUIDE_Y2026M09D25.pdf`, pp. 3-4) says transfers from bilateral sources through other
  channels are eliminated to avoid double counting. Its example code starts with `dah_db <- dah_db[elim_ch == 0]`.
- In the raw file, 166,895 of 1,475,413 rows have `elim_ch == 1`. Across 2021-2023 they hold US$40.6B of the US$238.3B
  recorded.

**Fix.** `prepare_model.py` now loads `elim_ch` and keeps only rows with `elim_ch == 0` immediately after reading the
raw file, before any aggregation.

## Result (matches the expected values)

- **Bucket totals now match IHME's health-focus-area totals** (70 countries, 2021-23 average):

  | Bucket | Difference from IHME |
  |---|---|
  | HIV | -0.03% |
  | TB | -0.06% |
  | Malaria | -0.07% |
  | Immunization | 0.00% |

- **Per country x bucket**, 224 of 280 cells are within 0.5%. The other 56 differ by at most US$42K a year. These are
  IHME's own rounding, not a modelling difference:
  - IHME rounds each program-area column to US$1,000 per row, separately from the bucket total.
  - Across all recipients' 2021-23 rows, TB program columns sum US$2.7M below `tb_dah_23` and malaria columns
    US$4.0M below (three-year totals).
  - Example: Fiji TB, US$20K vs US$24K a year.
  - The new check therefore passes when the gap is within 0.5% or US$50K a year. All 280 pass.
- **Baseline change:** Kenya -5.7%, Nigeria -6.8%, Ethiopia -8.3%, Somalia -46.5% (all as expected); Honduras -7.3%,
  Mozambique -5.1%; all countries -7.1% (US$11.13B to US$10.33B a year).
- **Other model inputs:** only the three DAH-derived columns of `country_inputs.csv` changed. The regressions moved
  slightly:
  - DTP3 coverage on vaccine aid: +1.06 to +1.27.
  - Poisson malaria: -0.093 to -0.099.
  - The fiscal-replacement estimate is unchanged: it uses IHME's spending database, not the DAH file.
- **Tests:** 160 of 160 pass. `tests/baseline_sudden.json` was regenerated locally at commit 5eb6111 with the rebuilt
  inputs.

**Why the IHME 2025 preset goes up.**
- That preset applies IHME's global 2025 / 2021-23 change for each donor x channel x disease (`ihme_trend.csv`).
- Before the fix, both years included the double-counted transfers. They were large in the 2025 preliminary rows for US
  bilateral HIV aid, so US bilateral HIV appeared to *rise* 12.5% in 2025 (ratio 1.125).
- With the duplicates removed it *falls* 39% (ratio 0.614): US$3.34B in 2021-23 against US$2.05B in 2025, consistent
  with the 2025 US cuts.
- Other cells moved less, for example US through NGOs HIV 0.60 to 0.57, Gates through NGOs malaria 0.88 to 0.77.
- So the old preset understated the 2025 US HIV cut; the new figure is the corrected one.

## Checks added to `audit/checks.py`

- `modelled_aid_equals_IHME_bucket_total_elim_ch0`: for every country x bucket, modelled aid equals IHME's
  `hiv/tb/mal/nch_cnv_dah_23` total for 2021-23 with `elim_ch == 0`, within 0.5% or US$50K a year (IHME rounding).
  - It reads the raw DAH file when present, else `country_data` (identical to the filtered file).
  - A companion row records how many cells exceed a strict 0.5%.
- `section1_dah_equals_section2_dah_same_year`: Section 1 DAH (IHME spending database) equals Section 2 DAH (IHME DAH
  database, `elim_ch == 0`) for every country and year shown (2015-2023), within 2%.
  - 624 of 630 country-years are within 2%.
  - The 6 outside (Mauritius 2015/16/18, Venezuela 2015/17, Libya 2015; at most 4.4%) are small differences between the
    two IHME products. They are unrelated to `elim_ch`, which Section 2 already applies.

**Full check run after the fix:** 39,065 rows (`checks_summary.md`).
- Every check that passed before still passes, and both new checks pass (70 of 70; 624 of 630).
- The only changes are in the aid-gain display issue I-15, which now touches 25 countries in the IHME 2025 preset
  instead of 10. The corrected 2025 ratios have more cells where aid rises (183 of 572, mostly immunization).
